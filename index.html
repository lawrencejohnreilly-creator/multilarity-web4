The MultilarityGrowth goes superhuman. The locus stays plural.

The Singularity is a story about one lineage that outgrows every other and owns everything downstream. This is the other shape. Capability accrues in the couplings — between many participants, humans included — and the frontier is an envelope over them rather than the path of any one.

Plurality is not what happens by default when many actors exist at once. That is the opening condition of every consolidation in history. It has to be held open, and holding it open is engineering work. This page does that work in the open, and reports when it is failing.

Section 4 · the five conditions

Necessary jointly, not severally

Satisfying four of five yields a delayed singleton rather than a plural ecology. Each verdict below is recomputed every interval from the observed state, never from a self-report.

Runtime

Eight agents. Seven measure, one repairs.

In autonomous mode the sentinel applies remediations as findings arrive. Switch to human oversight and it stops acting: findings queue for a person to approve or decline, and nothing changes until they do.

What this is

A reference instrument for a framework draft. The ecology it measures is a reference model, not a survey of real systems — the agents, indicators, records, and chain are the part meant to be reused. No threshold MI is specified here, deliberately: publishing one would invite Section 7.4 faster than any adversary could.
